     cannot be confirmed. Confirm forks `StrategyVersioningService.fork_semantic_update` with
     `StrategyChangeSource.CONVERSATION_CONFIRM`. Provenance is
     `strategy_version_conversation_links`.

     lessons, learning attribution, and RAG. Transcripts are not domain memory. READ_ONLY may
     write transcripts and drafts, not strategy version links.
