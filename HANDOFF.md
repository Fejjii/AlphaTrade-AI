## Root causes

1. Each Watcher scan built the eight-hour aggTrade window as raw rows and as `TradeEvent` objects, then kept that list on the snapshot, in hash intermediates, and in the raw cache.
2. `SymbolMonitorRuntime` appended every ingested trade for the process lifetime and kept the last batch and stream snapshot.
3. `ClosedAggTradeCache` could hold several full windows. Oversized windows are no longer stored.

These are retention costs, not a leak inside `TradeEvent` construction. Building and discarding trades does not climb RSS. Keeping them does.

## Before measurements

Clean process, measured 2026-09-28:

- Import baseline after `malloc_trim`: 41,291,776 bytes.
- 5,000 retained `TradeEvent`s: 51,589,120 bytes. Delta: +10,297,344 bytes (about 2.1 KB RSS per trade).
- An eight-hour tape of hundreds of thousands of trades is therefore hundreds of MB to over 1 GB if retained. Live counts were not measured.

## After measurements

Same clean process:

- After discarding the 5,000 trades and trimming: 44,146,688 bytes.
- Eight streamed reductions of 25,000 trades, RSS after each cycle: 44,257,280 then 44,261,376. Growth: 4,096 bytes.
- During-cycle RSS stayed on those same pages (44,257,280–44,265,472).
- Import of paper worker, Watcher, Telegram, assembler, and Binance modules: 107,220,992 bytes.
- Pytest's in-process stress RSS (about 225 MB) included earlier allocations and is not the worker baseline.

## Optimizations

- Stream aggTrades, hash them incrementally, accumulate closed-bar Decimal sums, store `ReleasedTradeTape`, set `trades=[]`.
- Cache the reduced snapshot, not the raw eight-hour payload.
- Trim the monitor book to `last_event_at` and clear it on reconnect.
- `gc.collect` plus glibc `malloc_trim` after each supervised cycle and after a reduction.
- Log `rss_bytes` / `rss_peak_bytes`. Prometheus gauges `process_resident_memory_bytes` and `process_resident_memory_peak_bytes`. Nullable BigInteger columns on `controlled_runtime_status`.

## Remaining risks

- Monitor lookback is still capped at 10 minutes and still builds `TradeEvent`s for that window.
- Render RSS if `malloc_trim` does not return pages.
- Unmeasured RSS of live Postgres, Redis, and Qdrant clients.
- Unknown live BTCUSDT print rate.
